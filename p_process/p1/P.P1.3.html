SCENARIO

Xiomara has an hour long commute from work. She forgot to by groceries over the weekend and didn't have any meals prepped. After searching for restaurants along her route home she found Get In My Bowl, locally owned Asian-fusion restaurant in Alexandria, VA known for their wide selection of noodle dishes. Xiomara places an online pickup order for their Drunken Noodles before she leaves work, so it will be ready by the time she arrives.
ACTOR
Xiomara
MOTIVATOR
Hour long commute from work
INTENTION
Xiomara wants to pick up dinner on her way home from work instead of buying ingredients or cooking.
ACTION
Xiomara searches online for a restaurant that's on her way home from work. She looks for places that offer online ordering and pickup services so she won't have to wait for her food to be prepared.
RESOLUTION
She finds Get In My Bowl, peruses their menu, and places an order for pickup. It is ready once she arrives, and she gets to enjoy her dinner without having to grab groceries.
